Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 24, 2026

At Handskill : PocketMates ("we," "our," or "us"), we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use our mobile application and related services.

1. Information Collection

We collect information to provide a better experience for our community. This includes:

  • Account Information: Email address, name, profile picture, and biography provided during registration.
  • User-Generated Content (UGC): Messages, photos, videos, and documents you share in chats or community feeds.
  • Real-Time Communication: Our video/audio calling feature uses WebRTC. Data is primarily peer-to-peer and is not stored on our servers.
  • Device Data: Technical information about your device (OS version, model) used for push notifications and app performance.

2. How We Use Data

We use the collected information for purposes including:

  • Enable messaging, calling, and community networking features.
  • Ensure community safety by monitoring for prohibited content.
  • Send important system updates and push notifications.
  • Improve app performance and fix technical bugs.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data. We only share information with trusted service providers:

  • Supabase: For secure database management and authentication.
  • Firebase: For reliable push notification delivery.

4. Safety and Moderation

To comply with App Store and Google Play Store regulations, we implement robust safety measures:

  • Reporting: Users can report any message or profile that violates our community standards.
  • Blocking: Users have the ability to block any other user instantly.
  • Enforcement: We reserve the right to remove content and terminate accounts that violate our zero-tolerance policy for objectionable content.

5. Account Deletion

You have the right to delete your account at any time within the app settings. Deleting your account will permanently remove your personal identity and profile data from our active systems.

If you have questions about this policy, please contact us at:
