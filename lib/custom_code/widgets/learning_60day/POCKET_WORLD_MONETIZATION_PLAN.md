## 📺 3. Tier 1: Free Tier Monetization (ആഡ്സ് വഴിയുള്ള വരുമാനം)

Free users enjoy complete access to English lessons and base gameplay without paying a single rupee, monetized through non-intrusive, value-exchange ads:

1. **Rewarded Video Ads (High eCPM: \$15 – \$35)**:
   - **Battle Lifeline Refill**: Watch a 30-second ad to unlock a `50-50` or `Hint` lifeline during tough Home Defense raids.
   - **Extra Daily Raids**: The standard limit is 2 raids/day. Users can watch a rewarded ad to unlock a 3rd or 4th bonus raid.
   - **Speed up Recovery**: Skip 12 hours of the 48-Hour Presidential Police Protection cooldown by watching an ad.
   - **FDC Bonus Boost**: Watch a rewarded video to double Fortress Defense Credits (FDC) earned from voice calls and chats.

2. **Smart Interstitial Ads (Frequency Capped)**:
   - Displayed only upon completing a full Daily Mission chapter (never during an ongoing duel or speech drill).

3. **Banner Ads**:
   - Subtle banner space at the base of the street view or inventory modals (Google AdMob / Unity Ads integration).
