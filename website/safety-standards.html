Child Safety Standards

Our Commitment to Community Safety

At Handskill : PocketMates, the safety of our community—especially our younger users—is our highest priority. We have implemented strict standards to prevent Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) on our platform.

1. Zero-Tolerance Policy

We maintain a zero-tolerance policy against any form of child exploitation. We strictly prohibit the creation, promotion, distribution, or storage of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM). Any account discovered attempting to engage in such activity will be permanently banned immediately.

2. Prohibited Content and Behavior

Prohibited activities include, but are not limited to:

  • The distribution of explicit images or videos involving minors.
  • Grooming behavior or any attempt to solicit sexual information from minors.
  • Harassment, bullying, or threatening behavior directed at children.
  • Content that promotes or normalizes the exploitation of children.

3. Reporting & Moderation Tools

PocketMates provides built-in tools for users to take action against unsafe content:

  • Report User: Users can report any profile that demonstrates suspicious or predatory behavior.
  • Report Message: Specific chat instances or media can be flagged for immediate review.
  • Rapid Response: Our moderation team reviews all safety reports within 24 hours and takes immediate action, including content removal and account termination.

4. Cooperation with Authorities

We comply with all relevant laws regarding child safety. In the event that CSAM or predatory behavior is detected, Handskill : PocketMates will report the user and the associated content to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and relevant local and national law enforcement authorities.
