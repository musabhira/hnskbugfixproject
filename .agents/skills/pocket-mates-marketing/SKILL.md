# Pocket Mates Instagram Marketing & Content Studio

This skill produces ready-to-record Reels scripts, scrap-book themed Carousel blueprints (ready for Canva/Figma), engaging captions, and growth strategies for **PoketMates**.

---

## 🎨 Visual Identity & Brand Rules
Whenever generating visual suggestions, carousels, or video frame concepts, strictly adhere to:
- Reference: `brand_voice.md`
- Background: Warm Craft Paper / Cardboard (`#E5D3BA`)
- Card Containers: Torn/Ripped Notebook Paper with binder ring holes and paper clips
- Accent Colors: Electric Yellow (`#FFFFFC00`), Mint Green pill tags, Deep Navy Slate (`#121B22`), Marker Rust Red
- Accents: Washi tapes on corners, playful stickers (smiley, stars, sun, heart), marker doodle arrows
- Tone: Friendly Manglish / Malayalam conversational hooks with punchy English practice

---

## 🎬 1. Reel Script Engine (`Reel / Short Video`)
When the user asks for a Reel or video script:
1. **Hook (0-3s)**: High-curiosity or relatable question in conversational Malayalam. Provide an on-screen text overlay in English/Manglish.
2. **The Problem / Relatable Stake (3-8s)**: Why traditional English learning failed them (fear, hesitation, boring grammar books).
3. **The Solution / Gamified Twist (8-25s)**: How PoketMates solves it (e.g., Level 20 Citadel, practicing with AI robots, 5 mins a day).
4. **CTA / The Loop (Last 5s)**: Clear call-to-action ("Comment 'MATE' or link in bio") with an echo loop back to the first word.

---

## 📑 2. Scrapbook Carousel Engine (`Carousel / Slides`)
When the user asks for a Carousel or multi-slide post:
1. **Slide 1 (Cover)**: Kraft background + Ripped sheet + Big bold headline (≤ 6 words) + Badge ("Learn with PoketMates").
2. **Slides 2 to N (Bite-sized Lessons)**: Clean two-column table (English phrase on left, Malayalam meaning on right with middle divider).
3. **Slide N+1 (The Cheat Sheet / Summary)**: Clean full list designed for viewers to save or screenshot.
4. **Final Slide (CTA)**: Mobile app mockup pinned with washi tape, showing the PoketMates download badge.

---

## ✍️ 3. Captions & Growth Keywords
Every generated script or carousel must include:
- **First 125 Characters**: Optimized before the "...more" button.
- **Micro-Value Body**: Bullet points expanding the topic.
- **Engagement Trigger**: "Save this for later! 📌" / "Comment 'START' to get the link".
- **Targeted Hashtags**: #PoketMates #LearnEnglishMalayalam #SpokenEnglishTips #MalayalamEnglish #EnglishVocabulary #KeralaStudents
