# Pocket Mates Brand Voice & Marketing Identity

This document defines the tone, style, positioning, and content rules for Pocket Mates social media marketing (Instagram Reels, Carousels, Stories, Captions).

---

## 1. Brand Essence
- **App Name**: PoketMates (Pocket Mates)
- **Tagline**: Learn. Build. Battle. | Speak English Every Day
- **Core Value Proposition**: The gamified Spoken English & AI companion app where learning English feels like playing a game (Level up, battle in Citadel, earn Pocket Scores, build your island, and chat with AI robots).
- **Target Audience**: 
  - College students, job seekers, and young professionals in Kerala / Malayalam-speaking diaspora who hesitate or fear speaking English in public.
  - Parents looking for a safe, gamified English learning platform for their kids.

---

## 2. Voice & Tone
- **Language Blend**: Natural Manglish / Malayalam conversational hooks + Clear, confident English sentences and explanations.
- **Vibe**: Friendly, encouraging, relatable, non-judgmental ("ഇംഗ്ലീഷ് സംസാരിക്കാൻ ഇനി പേടിക്കേണ്ട"). Never boring, academic, or condescending.
- **Personality**: A supportive best friend ("Mate") who cheers you on to level up your English every single day.
- **On Camera / Voiceover**: High-energy, warm, practical, relatable everyday situations (interviews, office chats, ordering food, talking to friends).
- **Words We Love**:
  - *Malayalam*: സിംപിൾ ആണ്, ഇംഗ്ലീഷ് പേടി മാറ്റാം, ലെവൽ അപ്പ്, പ്രാക്ടീസ് ചെയ്യാം, അടിപൊളി, ഡെയ്‌ലി 10 മിനിറ്റ്.
  - *English*: Level up, Pocket Score, Citadel battle, AI mate, speak fluently, everyday vocabulary.
- **Words We Never Use**: Complicated grammar jargon (gerunds, subjunctive mood, non-finite verbs), robotic corporate buzzwords, intimidating academic terms.

---

## 3. Visual & Aesthetic Guidelines (The "Scrapbook Craft" Theme)
Based on our verified brand poster references:
- **Background Base**: Warm Craft Paper / Cardboard texture (`#E5D3BA` / `#D4B895`).
- **Paper Sheet Elements**: Ripped/torn notepad paper sheets with binder holes or paper clips (`#FFFFFF` with subtle shadows).
- **Accent Colors**:
  - Primary Electric Yellow: `#FFFFFC00`
  - Mint / Cyan Teal: `#A8E6CF` / `#38BDF8`
  - Deep Navy Slate: `#121B22` / `#1E293B`
  - Rust / Marker Orange-Red: `#E05A47` (used for hand-drawn annotations like *"TODAY WAS A 20"*).
- **Typography**:
  - **Headlines**: Bold, warm serif or heavy slab serif ("Learn. Build. Battle.", "DAILY ENGLISH VOCABULARY").
  - **Subheadings**: Clean, modern sans-serif ("Learn with PoketMates", "English - Malayalam • Phrases with 'WHO'").
  - **Bullet Points**: Clean contrast table with divider lines.
  - **Handwritten Accents**: Marker-style doodle arrows, stars, stickers (sun, smiley, heart, rainbow, lightning).
- **Mobile Mockup Style**: Floating smartphone with dark border, dynamic notch, colorful sticky tapes (washi tape) at the corners, showing live gameplay / Citadel / Profile.

---

## 4. Key Pillars of Content
1. **Pillar 1: Quick English Vocabulary / Common Phrases (Daily High-Save Carousels)**
   - English to Malayalam comparative phrases (e.g., "Phrases with WHO", "How to say NO politely", "Office English vs Daily English").
   - Designed in the ripped notepad layout with a pencil icon and PoketMates watermark.
2. **Pillar 2: Gamification & App Features (Engagement & FOMO Reels)**
   - Showing gameplay: "Attacking your friend's Citadel because they didn't practice English today!"
   - "How I reached Level 20 on PoketMates while learning 200 words."
3. **Pillar 3: Real-life Relatable Situations (Reels with High Shares)**
   - "മലയാളികൾ ഇംഗ്ലീഷ് പറയുമ്പോൾ വരുത്തുന്ന 5 കോമൺ തെറ്റുകൾ" (5 Common Mistakes Malayalis make in English).
   - "ഇന്റർവ്യൂവിൽ 'Tell me about yourself' എന്ന് ചോദിച്ചാൽ എന്ത് പറയണം?"
